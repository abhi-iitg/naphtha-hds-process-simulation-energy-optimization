## Honest project-positioning answer

"This is a reproducible screening model. I built it to demonstrate the
engineering workflow and verify balances without proprietary software. A
production design would require rigorous thermodynamics, VLE, kinetics,
catalyst aging, hydraulics, detailed exchanger design, equipment sizing,
safety studies and validated plant data."
